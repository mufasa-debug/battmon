============================================================
  BATTMON: SUPER SIMPLE SETUP GUIDE (FOR ANY MAC)
============================================================

1. OPEN TERMINAL:
   • Press Cmd + Space on your keyboard.
   • Type "Terminal" and press Enter.

2. GO TO THIS FOLDER:
   • Type "cd " (with a space after cd).
   • Drag and drop this Battmon folder right into the Terminal window.
   • Press Enter.

3. INSTALL (ONE COMMAND):
   chmod +x setup.sh battmon battery_monitor.sh && ./setup.sh

4. CHOOSE YOUR SETUP:
   • Press 1 for Quick Install (Default alerts at 100%, 80%, 10%, 5%, 1%).
   • Or press 2 if you want to add custom percentages right away.

That's it! Battmon will now speak alerts in the background.
It automatically starts every time you turn on your Mac.

============================================================
  HOW TO USE IT
============================================================

Run any of these commands from any Terminal window:

• Open the settings menu:
  battmon

• Check your battery & see all your alerts:
  battmon status

• Test the voice and try out muting/charger stopping:
  battmon test

• Check system health and diagnostics:
  battmon doctor

• Pause or resume the background service:
  battmon stop
  battmon start

============================================================
  HOW TO STOP AN ALERT WHEN IT'S TALKING
============================================================

You have 4 easy ways to stop Battmon whenever it speaks:

1. Plug in your charger (stops low-battery alerts immediately).
2. Unplug your charger (stops full-charge alerts immediately).
3. Press the MUTE key (F10) on your keyboard.
4. Press the VOLUME DOWN key (F11) on your keyboard.

============================================================
  HOW TO UNINSTALL
============================================================

If you ever want to remove Battmon, open Terminal here and run:
  ./setup.sh --uninstall

Your custom alerts are automatically backed up.
To completely remove all files including backups, run:
  ./setup.sh --uninstall --purge
